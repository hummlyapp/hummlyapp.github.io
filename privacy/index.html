Privacy Policy

Effective November 26, 2026

Hummly is a calm home-cleaning helper. We built it to need as little of your data as possible: your home, rooms and tasks stay on your device.

This policy explains what the Hummly app for iPhone (“Hummly”, “we”) collects, why, and the choices you have. Hummly is made by Liudmyla Tymoshenko, an independent developer.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter into Hummly is stored only on your iPhone and is not sent to us:

  • your answers to the setup questions (who lives with you, your home, your time and reminder preferences);
  • your rooms, tasks, cleaning history and settings.

Reminders are local notifications scheduled by your iPhone. No server sends them.

What is sent to services we use

ServiceWhat it receivesWhy
Firebase Authentication (Google) A random anonymous account ID. No name, email or phone number. To recognize your app install, so your subscription and statistics stay linked to it.
Firebase Analytics (Google) Usage events, such as finishing setup, completing a task or opening the subscription screen, with simple details (for example, how many rooms were set up). Also device and app information that Analytics collects automatically: device model, iOS version, app version, language, and an approximate region derived from the IP address. To understand which parts of Hummly help people and where it can be improved.
RevenueCat The anonymous account ID and your subscription status and purchase history from the App Store. To check whether you have a subscription and unlock premium features.
Apple (App Store) Payments and subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple under your Apple Account. To process purchases. We never see your payment details.

Analytics events never include the names of your rooms or tasks, or any text you type.

What we do not do

  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not sell or share your data for advertising.
  • We do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and we do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.

How long data is kept

  • Data on your device stays until you delete the app.
  • Analytics event data is kept by Google for a limited period (up to 14 months) and then deleted automatically.
  • Subscription records are kept by RevenueCat and Apple for as long as needed for billing, accounting and legal obligations.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your data on the device: delete Hummly from your iPhone. This removes your rooms, tasks and history.
  • Delete data held by our services: email us at hummly.app@gmail.com. Since your account is anonymous, it helps to send the date you installed Hummly. We will delete the related analytics and subscription records that we control.
  • Manage or cancel a subscription: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
  • Notifications: turn them off at any time in iPhone Settings → Notifications → Hummly.

Depending on where you live (for example, in the EU, the UK or California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to object to its processing. Write to us and we will respond within 30 days. The legal basis for analytics is our legitimate interest in improving Hummly; subscription data is processed to perform our contract with you.

Children

Hummly is meant for adults managing a household. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

International transfers

Google and RevenueCat may process data in the United States and other countries. They use safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses where required.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app’s release notes.

Contact

Questions or requests: hummly.app@gmail.com

Що лишається на вашому пристрої

Усе, що ви вводите в Hummly, зберігається лише на вашому iPhone і не надсилається нам:

  • відповіді на запитання при налаштуванні (хто з вами живе, ваш дім, час і нагадування);
  • ваші кімнати, завдання, історія прибирання та налаштування.

Нагадування — це локальні сповіщення, які планує сам iPhone. Жоден сервер їх не надсилає.

СервісЩо отримуєНавіщо
